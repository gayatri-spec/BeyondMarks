BEYONDMARKS - INITIAL DATA INSIGHTS

Dataset:
UCI Student Performance - Mathematics

Students:
395

Initial Findings:

1. Study time:
Students with 5-10 hours/week had the highest average G3.
More study time did not automatically mean higher grades.

2. Absences:
There was no simple linear relationship between absence level
and final grade.

3. Previous failures:
Students with more previous failures had substantially lower
average final grades.

4. School support:
Students receiving school support had a lower average G3.
This should not be interpreted as support causing lower grades.

5. Family support:
The difference between students with and without family support
was relatively small.
